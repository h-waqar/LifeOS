# Plan 19-03: Real Mobile Audio Hardware, Ambient Noise Suppression & Speech Recognition Resilience

## Executive Summary
Validates and hardens the LifeOS Web Speech API voice capture engine under real-world mobile audio hardware variations and ambient background noise conditions:
1. Configures audio capture constraints for hardware microphone streams (`echoCancellation: true`, `noiseSuppression: true`, `autoGainControl: true`) to maximize speech clarity and filter background chatter in noisy environments (cafes, transit, outdoors).
2. Implements audio hardware input device enumeration and switching awareness (`navigator.mediaDevices.enumerateDevices`) supporting seamless transitions between built-in phone microphones, wired headsets, and Bluetooth audio devices (AirPods, Galaxy Buds).
3. Adds ambient noise resilience and automatic recovery: transient `no-speech` or ambient burst events do not fatally crash continuous dictation sessions; automatic restart/retry mechanics ensure seamless user dictation without manual restarts.
4. Provides clear, accessible visual and auditory feedback on audio capture health (active recording, background noise detected, microphone disconnected).
5. Reconciles Phase 19 planning state across ROADMAP, REQUIREMENTS, and STATE artifacts upon full phase verification.

## Objectives
1. **Audio Constraints & Hardware Noise Suppression (`src/hooks/use-speech-recognition.ts`)**:
   - Request microphone streams with standard noise suppression, acoustic echo cancellation, and automatic gain control when initializing audio context or testing microphone availability.
   - Detect available audio input devices via `navigator.mediaDevices.enumerateDevices` (when supported) and expose device selection / status.
2. **Transient Ambient Noise Recovery & Continuous Capture Resilience**:
   - Differentiate fatal errors (`not-allowed`, `audio-capture`) from transient noise-related events (`no-speech`, short network interruptions).
   - In continuous capture mode, automatically resume recognition if silenced by background noise or pauses, up to a configurable threshold, preserving accumulated transcripts.
3. **Accessible Noise & Hardware State Feedback (`src/components/voice/voice-dictation-button.tsx`)**:
   - Provide visual and screen-reader indicators for microphone status, recording level, and ambient noise warning.
4. **Planning State & Traceability Reconciliation**:
   - Reconcile `.planning/ROADMAP.md`: mark Phase 19 and plans 19-01, 19-02, 19-03 complete (`[x]`).
   - Reconcile `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md`: mark QA-01, QA-02, QA-03, QA-04, and PROD-02 satisfied (`[x]`).
   - Reconcile `.planning/STATE.md`: update active phase progress, metrics, and session continuity.
