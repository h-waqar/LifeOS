# Plan 19-01: Physical Handheld & Tablet Touch Ergonomics, Tap Target Bounds & Safe-Area Reflow

## Executive Summary
Implements and validates physical handheld smartphone and tablet ergonomics across LifeOS:
1. Configures standard mobile viewport metadata with `viewportFit: "cover"` to activate CSS safe-area insets (`env(safe-area-inset-top/bottom/left/right)`), preventing layout clipping under device notches, status bars, and physical/virtual home indicators on iOS and Android.
2. Enforces WCAG 2.5.5 / iOS HIG minimum 44x44px tap targets (`min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px]`) across primary interactive controls, icon buttons, and quick capture triggers.
3. Introduces an accessible, reachable Mobile Bottom Navigation Bar within natural one-handed thumb zones, providing instant access to Dashboard, Tasks, Quick Capture, AI Assistant, and Navigation Drawer on handheld viewports (`<768px`).
4. Ensures momentum scrolling (`-webkit-overflow-scrolling: touch`) and resilient virtual keyboard handling without viewport displacement or obscuring input fields.
5. Verifies responsive grid reflow for tablet form factors (iPad, Android tablets) across landscape/portrait orientations and split-view multitasking (50/50 and 70/30 viewports).

## Objectives
1. **Viewport & Safe-Area Inset Configuration (`src/app/layout.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`)**:
   - Export Next.js 15 `Viewport` with `viewportFit: "cover"`, `width: "device-width"`, `initialScale: 1`, `maximumScale: 5`, and theme colors.
   - Implement CSS utility and base rules for safe-area padding (`pb-safe`, `pt-safe`, `pl-safe`, `pr-safe`) and smooth momentum scrolling.
2. **Mobile Bottom Navigation & Thumb Zone Ergonomics (`src/components/app-shell.tsx`)**:
   - Add a fixed mobile bottom navigation bar (`md:hidden`) docked at the screen bottom with `pb-[env(safe-area-inset-bottom)]`.
   - Include primary thumb-accessible destinations: Dashboard, Tasks, Universal Quick Capture (prominent center action), AI Assistant, and Menu/Drawer toggle.
   - Ensure all touch targets in the bottom bar and mobile header meet or exceed 44x44px.
3. **Tap Target Bounds Hardening (`src/components/ui/button.tsx`, `src/components/quick-capture-modal.tsx`)**:
   - Ensure `Button` component and modal dismiss/action buttons have touch-accessible bounding boxes (`min-h-[44px] min-w-[44px]`) on touch interfaces.
   - Prevent accidental mistaps or unreachable controls near viewport boundaries.
4. **Virtual Keyboard & Modal Handling (`src/components/quick-capture-modal.tsx`)**:
   - Position Quick Capture modal appropriately for on-screen virtual keyboard appearance without displacing dialog actions or clipping input.
5. **Tablet Multitasking & Split-View Reflow**:
   - Verify layout adaptability across 768px–1024px tablet viewports, portrait/landscape orientation flips, and split-view widths (512px, 680px).
